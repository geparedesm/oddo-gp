# WhatsApp-Assisted Commercial Leasing Implementation Plan

> **For Hermes:** Use subagent-driven-development skill to implement this plan task-by-task.

**Goal:** Convert inbound WhatsApp enquiries from property signs into qualified commercial-lease applications while keeping legal, pricing, reservation, and contract approval under Property Manager control.

**Architecture:** Extend the existing public-property MCP flow with an Odoo-managed lead/application workflow. Hermes gathers consented prospect information through WhatsApp and calls narrowly scoped authenticated endpoints; it must never create an active lease, expose private information, or promise availability. Odoo remains the source of truth for availability, reservation, review, documents, payments, and final activation.

**Tech Stack:** Odoo 16 Community (`commercial_property_management`, `mail`, `contacts`), existing bearer-token public API and local MCP server, Hermes Gateway WhatsApp bridge, Node MCP SDK, Playwright, Odoo tests.

---

## Product framing

A sign can say: “Interested in renting? Write to WhatsApp.” The desired journey is not an automatic legal rental from a chat. It is a safe conversion funnel:

1. Prospect scans the unique QR code on the sign or opens its short link.
2. WhatsApp receives the prefilled internal property reference and Hermes presents public information without asking the prospect to identify the property.
3. Hermes collects permission to process contact information and basic commercial requirements.
4. Hermes creates a qualified enquiry/application in Odoo.
5. A Property Manager reviews the application, validates documents and commercial terms, and decides whether to reserve the property.
6. The manager creates or approves the lease. Only then does availability change to Reserved/Rented through existing lease automation.

This preserves the current public/private data boundary and prevents double booking, unauthorized price commitments, or accidental contract formation in WhatsApp.

## Brainstorm: candidate capabilities

### Highest-value, lowest-risk

- One unique QR code or short link per property sign that opens WhatsApp with a prefilled, internal property reference; the visitor never has to read or type a code.
- Conversational search by property code, city, type, area and monthly budget.
- “Request a visit” flow with preferred dates/time windows.
- “I want to rent this property” flow that records a lead and notifies a manager.
- Consent notice before capturing name, phone, company and business requirements.
- Manager activity, Kanban pipeline and response SLA for inbound enquiries.
- Automatic WhatsApp acknowledgement and manager-owned follow-up.

### Next conversion improvements

- Qualification questions: company/person, business activity, desired start date, lease duration, headcount, required services, budget.
- Availability disclaimer and price confirmation: “subject to manager approval and contract.”
- Property-specific brochure/media sent only from public assets.
- Appointment slots integrated with Odoo Calendar.
- Applicant document checklist and secure upload path.
- Reservation request with expiry, deposit policy and manager approval.

### Later-stage automation

- Configurable quote/proposal generated by manager from a reviewed application.
- E-signature through a legally appropriate provider for the target jurisdiction.
- Payment-link integration for an approved reservation/deposit.
- Contract onboarding, handover checklist and renewal messaging.
- Analytics: sign/QR → conversation → visit → application → reservation → active lease conversion.

## Guardrails and non-goals

- Do not set `WHATSAPP_ALLOWED_USERS=*` for a public sign. A public contact channel needs a deliberately designed onboarding/pairing or controlled lead-intake policy first.
- Do not create, activate, cancel, or modify a `commercial.lease` directly from WhatsApp.
- Do not expose tenant identities, contract terms, deposits, internal notes, activities, or unpublished inventory.
- Do not accept a WhatsApp message as legal acceptance, a binding quotation, or payment confirmation without jurisdiction-specific legal review.
- Do not store identity documents or payment data in WhatsApp chat history. Use a secure authenticated upload/payment flow.

## Phase 10: Multi-unit property structure

**Objective:** Represent one building/property with several independently rentable commercial units before automating WhatsApp enquiries.

The current `commercial.property` model represents one rentable listing. For a building with several storefronts, split the concepts:

- **Property/building:** shared address, city, owner/management data, common photos and public building description.
- **Commercial unit:** the actual rentable local. It has a human-facing label (for example, “Ground floor corner unit” or “Local next to the pharmacy”), optional suite/local number, floor, entrance/facade description, area, public rent, photos, availability and lease history.

Leases, reservations, leads and public listings must target the commercial unit, never the whole building. This prevents a lease for one local from marking every local in the building as rented.

**Files likely to change:**
- Create: `addons/commercial_property_management/models/commercial_property_unit.py`
- Modify: `addons/commercial_property_management/models/commercial_property.py`
- Modify: `addons/commercial_property_management/models/commercial_lease.py`
- Modify: `addons/commercial_property_management/controllers/hermes_api.py`
- Modify: property, lease and public-listing views, access rules, tests and MCP tools.

**Steps:**
1. Write failing model tests for two units in one building, each with independent rent, availability and lease state.
2. Add `commercial.property.unit` linked to `commercial.property`; use a human label and photos rather than forcing customers to type a technical code.
3. Move or duplicate the rentable fields to the unit level and make leases point to `unit_id`.
4. Preserve a migration path for existing one-property/one-unit records by creating one default unit per current property.
5. Update public search to return available units with their building address/city and public unit description.
6. Update Odoo views so managers see a building with its units and each unit's lease history.
7. Add E2E coverage proving an active lease on Unit A does not hide or rent Unit B.

**Acceptance criteria:** Managers can lease units independently within one building, and Hermes can present the right local without treating the complete property as unavailable.

### Task 1: Confirm business and legal policy before implementation

**Objective:** Turn the open questions below into explicit acceptance criteria.

**Steps:**
1. Choose the operating jurisdiction and identify the legal status of a WhatsApp enquiry, reservation and signed lease.
2. Approve the consent wording, privacy-retention period and allowed lead fields.
3. Define the approved manager response SLA and visit hours.
4. Decide whether unknown WhatsApp users use a pairing workflow, an approved channel, or a public web form linked from the QR code.
5. Record the decisions in `docs/whatsapp-lead-intake.md`.

**Acceptance criteria:** A written policy states that chat does not form a lease and identifies who can approve reservations and contracts.

### Task 2: Add a minimal Odoo enquiry model

**Objective:** Persist an auditable prospect request without creating a tenant or lease prematurely.

**Data model:**
- `commercial.property.lead` with `property_id`, `name`, `phone`, `company_name`, `email`, `business_activity`, `desired_start_date`, `message`, `consent_at`, `source`, `state`, `assigned_user_id`, and `visit_requested_at`.
- States: `new`, `qualified`, `visit_scheduled`, `under_review`, `rejected`, `converted`.
- Store the WhatsApp chat identifier only if necessary for follow-up, limit it to managers, and redact it from public endpoints.

**Steps:**
1. Write tests proving required consent/property validation and state transitions fail when invalid.
2. Implement the model with `mail.thread` and `mail.activity.mixin`.
3. Add manager-only ACLs/record rules and confirm Property Users cannot read leads.
4. Schedule a manager activity when a new lead is created.
5. Run `./scripts/test-module-logic.sh commercial_property_management`.

**Acceptance criteria:** A manager can review a lead in Odoo; a public API client cannot read leads; no lead automatically creates a tenant, reservation, or lease.

### Task 3: Build manager lead views and queue

**Objective:** Give managers a simple operational queue for WhatsApp enquiries.

**Steps:**
1. Add list, Kanban, form and search views for `commercial.property.lead`.
2. Add filters for New, Unassigned, Visit Requested and Overdue.
3. Add a manager-only menu under Commercial Properties.
4. Add activities/chatter and buttons for Qualify, Schedule Visit, Reject and Convert to Tenant Draft.
5. Add Playwright coverage for manager visibility and Property User denial.

**Acceptance criteria:** Managers can triage an enquiry in fewer than three clicks and can see its linked property, consent and follow-up activity.

### Task 4: Add a narrow lead-submission API and MCP tool

**Objective:** Allow Hermes to submit a consented enquiry for a property code already returned from public search.

**Steps:**
1. Write controller tests for missing/invalid bearer tokens, unknown/unpublished property codes, missing consent and successful lead creation.
2. Add `POST /api/hermes/properties/<property_code>/enquiries` with strict field allowlisting, rate limiting and no response that reveals internal lead data.
3. Add an MCP tool such as `submit_property_enquiry` that requires property code, name, phone and consent confirmation.
4. Require Hermes to present a non-binding availability disclaimer before invoking the tool.
5. Run `npm run test:hermes-mcp` and endpoint tests.

**Acceptance criteria:** Hermes can create an enquiry only after explicit consent and returns a neutral confirmation, never a promise of reservation or contract.

### Task 5: Identify the building and unit with simple conversation

**Objective:** Identify the right commercial unit without asking the prospect to scan a QR code, share live location or type a technical code.

**Steps:**
1. Ask for a human reference such as street, zone, nearby business or building name when a prospect writes from a generic sign.
2. When multiple units exist in the same building, present no more than three public options using a human-facing unit name, photo, facade/entrance description, area and rent.
3. Accept natural responses such as “the corner one”, “next to the pharmacy” or “the larger one”; ask a single confirmation question if needed.
4. Add the chat disclaimer: “Availability, price and any lease are subject to manager review and signed contract.”
5. Test an unambiguous reference, an ambiguous building with several units, a discontinued listing and a no-match response.

**Acceptance criteria:** A prospect reaches the correct commercial unit through a short natural conversation, without a QR code, live-location request or technical code.

## Phase 12: Qualification, appointments and controlled reservation

**Objective:** Improve conversion from lead to manager-approved reservation without double booking.

**Files likely to change:**
- Create: `addons/commercial_property_management/models/commercial_property_visit.py`
- Create: `addons/commercial_property_management/models/commercial_property_reservation.py`
- Modify: `addons/commercial_property_management/models/commercial_property.py`
- Modify: `addons/commercial_property_management/models/commercial_lease.py`
- Create/modify corresponding views, ACLs, tests, MCP tool tests and E2E tests.

### Task 1: Add a visit-request workflow

**Steps:**
1. Model visit requests with property, lead, requested time windows, assigned manager, confirmed date and state.
2. Create manager activities and optionally sync confirmed events to Odoo Calendar.
3. Expose only a request operation to Hermes; managers alone confirm appointments.
4. Test timezone handling, cancellation and no-show paths.

### Task 2: Add a reservation request, not an automatic reservation

**Steps:**
1. Define reservation states: requested, manager-approved, expired, cancelled, converted.
2. Enforce one active approved reservation per property and an explicit expiry date.
3. Change property state to Reserved only after manager approval, reusing the existing availability rules safely.
4. Create activities before reservation expiry and return the property to Available when it expires.
5. Test conflicts with active/future leases and concurrent approvals.

**Acceptance criteria:** A prospect cannot reserve a property through chat alone; managers can approve a time-limited reservation without conflicting with leases.

## Phase 13: Application, documents, quote and contract handoff

**Objective:** Move a qualified/reserved prospect through a compliant manager-owned application process.

**Steps:**
1. Define an application checklist appropriate to the jurisdiction and applicant type.
2. Build a secure, authenticated document-upload channel; do not accept sensitive documents through WhatsApp.
3. Add manager review/approval states and audit trail.
4. Generate a non-binding proposal/quote from approved terms.
5. Allow a manager to create a draft lease from a reviewed application; retain manual activation controls.
6. Evaluate electronic-signature and payment providers only after legal and security review.

**Acceptance criteria:** Every active lease is traceable to a reviewed application and manager approval; no payment card or identity document is stored in WhatsApp.

## Phase 14: Acquisition analytics and operational hardening

**Objective:** Measure sign-to-lease conversion and protect the public automation channel.

**Steps:**
1. Capture source attribution: sign/QR/property code/campaign.
2. Add dashboard measures for enquiries, response SLA, visits, reservations, conversion and lost reasons.
3. Add rate limits, abuse detection, idempotency keys and retention/anonymization jobs for WhatsApp leads.
4. Add an authorization workflow for WhatsApp contacts; never open the administrative Hermes toolset to arbitrary public senders.
5. Add alerting for MCP/API failures and queue backlogs.

**Acceptance criteria:** Managers can see which signs generate qualified leads, and the public channel is monitored, rate-limited and privacy-compliant.

## Phase 15: Property operations and maintenance

**Objective:** Manage the physical condition of every independently rentable unit after it is listed, reserved or leased.

**Capabilities:**
- Maintenance tickets per building and per commercial unit.
- Categories for inspection, damage, cleaning, utilities, repair and preventive maintenance.
- Assignment to an internal owner or external provider, due dates, cost estimate, completion evidence and audit trail.
- Manager dashboard for units unavailable because of maintenance and units awaiting handover inspection.
- Delivery/return checklist with photos and notes; private evidence remains unavailable to Hermes and public APIs.

**Files likely to change:**
- Create: `addons/commercial_property_management/models/commercial_property_maintenance.py`
- Create: corresponding views, actions, ACLs, record rules and tests.
- Modify: `addons/commercial_property_management/models/commercial_property.py` and the future unit model to show maintenance history and operational status.
- Modify: `docs/lease-operations.md`.

**Acceptance criteria:** A manager can open, assign, close and audit maintenance work without exposing operational notes or photos to public listing/WhatsApp flows.

## Phase 16: Financial operations and portfolio performance

**Objective:** Give managers a reliable view of expected income, collection status, vacancy and performance by building and commercial unit.

**Capabilities:**
- Approved commercial terms: deposit, rent adjustments, penalties and renewal conditions, all manager-controlled.
- Optional Odoo Accounting integration for invoices, payment status and overdue reminders.
- Portfolio measures: occupancy rate, vacancy days, average rent, expected monthly income, delinquency and renewal rate.
- Manager-only reporting by building, unit, zone, property type and lead source.
- Controlled payment-link evaluation only after selecting a compliant provider; never collect card/payment details in WhatsApp.

**Files likely to change:**
- Modify: `addons/commercial_property_management/models/commercial_lease.py`.
- Create: financial/reporting models only if Odoo Accounting cannot provide the required measures directly.
- Modify: dashboard views, manager ACLs, reporting tests and administrator documentation.

**Acceptance criteria:** Managers can identify vacant, overdue and high-performing units from Odoo while all payment and accounting access remains restricted to authorized roles.

## Phase 17: Public listing quality and controlled distribution

**Objective:** Improve demand generation without widening the public/private data boundary.

**Capabilities:**
- Public listing checklist: high-quality public photos, human-facing unit name, area, rent, features and non-sensitive location description.
- Publication status with manager approval, publication/expiry dates and reason for unpublishing.
- Optional controlled publication to a website, property portals or social campaigns through approved connectors.
- Attribution by building/unit/sign/campaign, feeding the Phase 14 conversion dashboard.
- Reusable WhatsApp responses for photos, FAQs, viewing preparation and manager handoff.

**Acceptance criteria:** Only manager-approved, available units are distributed publicly, and managers can measure which listing source creates qualified visits and leases.
